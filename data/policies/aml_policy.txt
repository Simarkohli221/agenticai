AML TRANSACTION MONITORING POLICY

Suspicious transaction activity may include unusual transaction amounts,
rapid movement of funds, transactions involving multiple accounts,
unusual payment patterns, and activity inconsistent with the expected
customer profile.

Transactions identified as potentially suspicious must be reviewed
by an authorized investigator.

High-risk cases require human review before any consequential account
action is taken.

Investigation records must contain the relevant transaction details,
risk indicators, supporting policy references, and the investigator's
decision.

Financial records must not be permanently deleted as part of an
investigation workflow. Account status changes should preserve the
historical record.
